# Apple — Style Reference (with SensaCine Cinema & Tertiary Blue Extensions)
> A photography-first interface that turns marketing and cinema into a museum gallery. Edge-to-edge product tiles alternate light and dark canvases, framed by SF Pro Display headlines with negative letter-spacing and a signature Action Blue (#0071e3) interactive color, supplemented with subtle cinematic tertiary blues for badges, glows, and experience tiers. UI chrome recedes so the film artwork and experiences can speak.

**Theme:** Dark & Light adaptive (Cinema Dark primary)

**Source reference:** [https://www.apple.com/](https://www.apple.com/) / Apple TV+ UI

## Tokens — Typography

### SF Pro Display / Inter · `--font-primary`
- **Substitute:** Inter, system-ui, sans-serif
- **Weights:** 700, 600, 500, 400
- **Sizes:** 56px (Hero), 40px (Display LG), 28px (Lead), 21px (Tagline), 17px (Body), 14px (Caption), 12px (Fine print)

## Tokens — Spacing & Radius

- **Radius:** sm (`8px`), md (`11px`), lg (`18px`), xl (`24px`), pill (`9999px`)
- **Card Padding:** `24px`
- **Section Gap:** `64px` - `80px`
- **Backdrop Blur:** `20px` (Frosted glass navigation and floating action bars)
